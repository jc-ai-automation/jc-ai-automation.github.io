About

01

I'm an AI Automation Specialist focused on designing workflows and AI agents that reduce manual work and connect the tools teams already rely on. My work centers on making systems talk to each other cleanly, through APIs, webhooks, and event based automation, so information moves without someone having to move it by hand.

I'm particularly interested in the space where automation platforms meet conversational AI: building agents that don't just respond, but complete real tasks like qualifying leads, screening candidates, or monitoring operations end to end.
